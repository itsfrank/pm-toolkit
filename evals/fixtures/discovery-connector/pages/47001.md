# FY27 cost per TB-month plan

Planning numbers for the FY27 Warehouse storage budget. Not a pricing input: the pricing committee sets list prices.


## Blended hot + cold, FY27 plan
| AWS | USD per TB-month | Note |
|---|---|---|
| us-east-1 | 21.40 | baseline |
| eu-central-1 | 25.25 | 18% above, mostly a smaller committed-use discount |
| Dublin | 9.80 | cold tier only |

That figure assumes 40 accounts there by the end of FY27. Below 15
the committed-use discount does not apply and the premium rises to about 31%.

## What this page is not
It says nothing about where any account's events live today.
