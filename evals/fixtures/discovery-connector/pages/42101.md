# Collect regional edge (code name Skerry)

Status: **Proposed.** Design review scheduled 2026-10-14. Not approved and not on the
committed roadmap. Tech lead: Rafael Osei. PM: Wen Liu.

## Problem
All edge receivers and the spool run in us-east-1, so events for Frankfurt-homed tenants
are received and buffered in the US before they reach their Warehouse. Several Enterprise
prospects ask for ingestion that never leaves the EU.

## Plan
- Run edge receivers and a spool in eu-central-1 for EU-homed tenants, behind a regional
  hostname (eu.ingest.kestrel.example).
- SDKs pick the hostname from the tenant's home region; no customer configuration.
- Raw request logs (client IPs, 14 days) for these tenants also move to eu-central-1.

## Dates
- Pilot with 2 design-partner tenants: target **2027-04-30**. Engineering target only;
  not approved for external communication.
- Earlier drafts said December 2026; that date was dropped on 2026-09-30 after capacity
  planning with Infrastructure.

## Open questions
- Who pays for the second spool cluster (about $210k/year)? Pricing & Packaging.
- Does the abuse-handling team need access to EU request logs from the US?
