# Infrastructure decision log

Decisions about Warehouse storage, hosting regions, snapshots, backup and disaster recovery,
and capacity. Each decision is a child page of this page, named `D-YYYY-NN: <topic>`.

Rules:
- A decision is **Accepted** only when the EM has signed it on the child page.
- Superseded decisions stay, marked Superseded, with a link to the new one.
- Older decisions (before 2026) live in the PROD space decision records.
