# D-2026-19: Snapshot copy site for Frankfurt-homed tenants

Status: **Accepted** (Tomas Lindqvist, 2026-10-02). Proposed by Mei Arakawa.

## Context
Every Warehouse tenant gets a nightly snapshot, and each snapshot is copied to a second
site so we can restore after losing a whole region. Today the copy site is N. Virginia for every tenant, including the design-partner tenants homed in Frankfurt
during the beta. Nobody revisited the copy site when the Frankfurt region was built.

## Decision
- From **2026-11-16**, nightly snapshots of Frankfurt-homed tenants are copied to Dublin instead of N. Virginia.
- Copies of Frankfurt-homed tenant snapshots already in N. Virginia are deleted 35 days
  after the switch (by 2026-12-21).
- Restore drills for Frankfurt-homed tenants run monthly from December 2026.

## Consequences
- Adds an estimated $60k/year to the Frankfurt region run-rate.
- Until 2026-11-16, snapshot copies of Frankfurt-homed tenants are stored in N. Virginia.
- Support and Legal informed on 2026-10-02.
