# Germany office move: visas, HR steps

For employees moving to the office in Germany, opened in 2026.

## Before you move
- Ask your HR partner about the Blue Card or a national visa: six to ten weeks.
- Moving budget depends on level, family size; your HR partner has the table.
- Register your address within two weeks of arrival.
