# Edge receiver runbook

Edge receivers run in us-east-1 only (three availability zones) behind ingest.kestrel.example.

## Alerts
- Accept p99 over 120 ms for 10 minutes: scale out the receiver group.
- Spool depth over 30 minutes of traffic: check router lag and Warehouse writers.

## Request logs
Raw request logs are kept 14 days for abuse handling, then deleted.
