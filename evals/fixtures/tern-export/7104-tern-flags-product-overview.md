# Tern Flags product overview

Tern Flags lets teams ship features behind flags and roll them out by percentage, segment
or region.
- Evaluation happens in the SDK with rules cached for 60 seconds.
- About 60 customer workspaces.
- Owner: Flags team (PM Ola Brandt, EM Keiko Tan).
