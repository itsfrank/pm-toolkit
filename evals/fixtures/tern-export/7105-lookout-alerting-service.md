# Lookout alerting service

Lookout is Tern's internal anomaly-alerting service. It watches capture-API error rates and
session volume per workspace and pages the on-call engineer when they drift.

- Runs in us-west-2.
- Owner: Replay platform team (Ivo Marsh).
- Not customer-facing.
