# Data retention policy

- Session recordings are kept for 90 days in AWS us-west-2, then deleted.
- Enterprise workspaces can choose 30, 90 or 180 days.
- When a workspace is deleted, its recordings are deleted within 30 days.
- Flags evaluation logs are kept for 14 days.
