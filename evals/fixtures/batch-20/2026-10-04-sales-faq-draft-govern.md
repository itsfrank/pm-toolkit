## About this FAQ
The aim is a one-page set of answers that sellers can use in early Govern conversations. It covers positioning, packaging, security and common objections.

## Q: What is Kestrel Govern?
Kestrel Govern is the access-control and audit layer for Kestrel. It lets admins define roles, restrict access by location and review an audit log of who did what.

## Q: Is Govern available today?
Kestrel Govern is generally available. Customers can ask their account manager to switch it on for their tenant.

## Q: Which plans include it?
Packaging is still being discussed, so sellers should check with Pricing & Packaging before quoting. (Draft placeholder.)

## Q: How does Govern compare to competitors?
Orbit Metrics offers workspace roles but, in our understanding, not location-based access rules. Sellers should say that Govern is designed for regulated industries, and avoid specific claims about competitor features until verified.

## Q: Does Govern support audit export?
Customers can view and export the audit log. Details on scheduled exports are not final. (Draft placeholder.)

## Q: What do I say to a customer in a regulated industry?
Lead with role-based access and the audit trail. Ask what regulations apply, and offer a security briefing with the Govern team.

## Q: Can customers pilot Govern?
Yes, through the programme run by Customer Success. Sellers should introduce the customer to their CSM.

## Q: What objections should sellers expect?
- "We already have role-based access in another tool." Response: ask what that tool covers for dashboards and exports, and position Govern as native to Kestrel, so there is nothing to keep in sync.
- "Is the audit log tamper-proof?" Response: do not claim this until Security has reviewed wording; offer a briefing instead.
- "How long is the audit log kept?" Response: retention depends on plan; check with Product before quoting any figure. (Draft placeholder.)
- "Can we get it in the EU?" Response: residency is a separate topic handled by the Warehouse team; refer the customer to their CSM.
