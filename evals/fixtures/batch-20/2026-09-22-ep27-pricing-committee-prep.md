Owner: Marco Bellini (Pricing & Packaging) | Status: draft, not approved | Updated: 2026-09-22

These notes collect what I plan to bring to the pricing committee on the Enterprise Plus add-on (EP-27). They are working notes for the committee prep, not a decision.

## Background
Enterprise customers keep asking for a bundle of premium capabilities: longer retention, priority support and the upcoming Govern features. Today these are sold through custom quotes, which makes deals slow and discounts inconsistent. EP-27 proposes a single add-on so Sales has one price to quote.

## Proposal
The Enterprise Plus add-on is priced at an 18% uplift on the Enterprise list price. This is a proposal only; it has not been approved by the committee or by Finance, and Sales should not quote it.

What the add-on would contain (still being refined):
- Priority support with a faster response target.
- Extended retention options.
- Early access to Govern capabilities once they reach general availability.
- A named technical contact.

## Options considered
| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Single add-on, percentage uplift | Simple to quote, scales with account size | Hard to relate to cost drivers |
| B | Flat fee per feature | Transparent | Many SKUs, complex contracts |
| C | Keep custom quotes | Flexible | Slow, inconsistent discounting |

I recommend option A. Finance prefers it because it is easy to model.

## Timing
The pricing committee decision moved to 2026-12-10; it was planned for November 2026. The move gives Finance time to complete the margin analysis and lets us see Govern's cost data from the load test. Communications plans depend on the decision, so nothing customer-facing should be scheduled before it.

## Open questions
1. How does the add-on interact with multi-year discounts?
2. Does it apply per tenant or per contract?
3. How should existing custom-quote customers be migrated, and when?
4. What does the add-on mean for Team plan customers who upgrade?

## Competitive context
Orbit Metrics bundles premium support into its top tier, so a separate add-on may look more expensive on a price sheet. Sales enablement will need a comparison that explains the difference. This is a qualitative view; I have not collected new price data.
