# Org update: Product and Engineering changes

From: Dana Okafor, VP Product | Date: 2026-09-24 | To: Product and Engineering, all hands

Hi everyone,

I want to share some changes to how we are organised for the next two quarters. These changes are aimed at lining up product leadership with where our customers are heading, especially Govern and Insights.

## Product changes
- Jonah Feld moves from Insights PM to Govern PM effective 2026-10-15. Jonah has led Insights through the v2 launch and the migration planning, and he is the right person to carry Govern towards general availability.
- Aisha Karim becomes Group PM for Govern and Insights. Aisha will work closely with Jonah and set shared priorities across the two products.

## Engineering changes
- Kai Lund is acting EM for Insights until 2027-01-11. Kai has been tech lead on the dashboard editor and knows the team well. Bea Santos remains part of the Insights team during this period but will not be managing day to day; please send engineering management questions for Insights to Kai.
- Lukas Brenner continues as Govern EM. No changes to the Govern engineering team.

## Why now
Govern and Insights are converging: Govern roles already apply to Insights dashboards, and several customers (including Enterprise accounts) buy both. A shared group product role reduces hand-offs and makes trade-offs between the two products visible earlier. We also want a smoother transition for Insights v1 customers who are moving to v2 while adopting Govern.

## Transition plan
| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2026-09-24 | This announcement |
| Next two weeks | Jonah and Aisha run joint handover sessions with the Insights and Govern teams |
| 2026-10-15 | Jonah's Govern PM role begins |
| Quarter end | Review of how the group structure is working |

## What does not change
- Sam Whitlock remains PM for Usage Alerts and reports as before.
- Marco Bellini continues to lead Pricing & Packaging.
- Roadmap commitments made to customers stay as they are. If you have questions about a specific commitment, ask Aisha.

## Practical notes
- Customer-facing docs and the product pages will be updated with new owners by the end of next week.
- Jira component owners will be updated by the respective teams.
- Customer Success: if you have introductions planned for Insights customers, loop in both Jonah and Aisha.

## Thanks
Thank you to Jonah, Aisha, Kai and Bea for their flexibility, and to all of you for supporting the transition. I will take questions at the next all hands and in my office hours on Thursday.

Dana

P.S. Bea Santos is on parental leave, which is the reason for the acting role. Please congratulate her when she is back, and give Kai your support in the meantime.
