# Platform on-call and incident severity policy

Owner: Tomas Lindqvist (Infrastructure EM) with Priya Natarajan and Lukas Brenner. Status: **Policy v3, effective 2026-11-02**. Applies to all Kestrel production services.

## Why this page
Incidents were handled differently by each team in FY26. This policy sets one severity scale, one on-call model and one communications cadence.

## Severity definitions
| Level | Definition | Acknowledge within | Status page update | Command |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Full outage or data loss/exposure for many tenants | 5 min | Every 30 min | Incident commander + exec notified |
| SEV2 | Major feature down or severe degradation for some tenants | 15 min | Every 60 min | Incident commander |
| SEV3 | Minor degradation, workaround exists | 1 hour | Daily | On-call owns |
| SEV4 | Cosmetic or single-tenant, no data impact | Next business day | None | Ticket |

Severity is set by the *first responder* and may be raised by anyone, lowered only by the incident commander.

### Examples
- SEV1: Collect accepts events but Warehouse writes are dropping them for all tenants.
- SEV2: Insights dashboards fail for Enterprise tenants in one region.
- SEV3: Govern audit lag over 5 minutes (see the audit runbook).
- SEV4: Wrong tooltip text in the Insights editor.

## On-call model
- One primary and one secondary per team. Rotations are weekly, **handoff every Tuesday at 10:00 UTC**.
- Follow-the-sun is not in use; Lisbon and US-East share the secondary slot.
- On-call engineers get **a day in lieu after a week with 3 or more night pages**.
- Compensation is covered in the People handbook, not here.

## Roles in an incident
| Role | Does | Does not |
|---|---|---|
| Incident commander (IC) | Decides severity, assigns roles, owns comms cadence | Debug |
| Operations lead | Debugs and mitigates | Talk to customers |
| Communications lead | Updates status page and Support | Debug |
| Scribe | Keeps the timeline | Decide |

**IC training: all on-call engineers must complete IC training by 2026-11-30.** Engineers without it are removed from the primary rotation from 2026-12-01.

## Timeline expectations (SEV1)
1. T+0: alert fires or report arrives; first responder acknowledges.
2. T+5 min: acknowledgement SLA; IC named; channel `#inc-<date>-<slug>` opened.
3. T+15 min: first status page post, "investigating".
4. Every 30 minutes: update until mitigated.
5. Within 24 hours of resolution: draft timeline posted.
6. **Postmortem published within 5 business days** for SEV1 and SEV2.

## Postmortem rules
- Blameless; focus on systems and detection.
- Required sections: summary, impact, timeline, root cause, what went well, action items with owners and dates.
- Action items go into the owning team's tracker with label `postmortem`.
- Review meeting within 7 days; Tomas Lindqvist signs off on SEV1.

## Communication with customers
- Support (Tier 2/3) owns outbound customer mail; engineering supplies facts.
- Do not state a root cause externally before the postmortem is reviewed.
- Hosting location claims must come from the approved answer page, not from incident text.

## Tooling
- Pager: company paging tool (fictional name: BeeperOps). Status page: hosted, owned by Support.
- Lookout, the support console, has an "incident banner" setting used by Support.
- Incident channels are archived after 30 days.

## Exceptions
Teams may request a stricter policy (for Govern audit data, for example) but not a looser one. Exceptions are recorded in the log below.

## FAQ
**Who can call a SEV1?** Anyone on call. **What if two incidents overlap?** Separate ICs. **Do planned maintenances count?** No, unless they exceed their window by 30 minutes.

## Appendix B: handover checklist
1. Read the previous primary's handover note in `#platform-oncall`.
2. Review open incidents and postmortem action items due within 7 days.
3. Test page yourself; confirm phone and push notifications.
4. Confirm access: production read-only, deploy tool, Lookout, status page.
5. Note planned maintenance windows for the week.

## Appendix C: status page wording templates
- Investigating: "We are investigating reports of <symptom> affecting <scope>. Next update in 30 minutes."
- Identified: "We have identified the cause and are working on a fix."
- Monitoring: "A fix has been applied. We are monitoring."
- Resolved: "This incident has been resolved. A summary will follow."
Never name a hosting region, vendor or tenant in status text.

## Appendix D: severity decision tree
- Is customer data lost, corrupted or exposed? If yes, SEV1.
- Is a core path (ingest, query, login) failing for more than one tenant? If yes, SEV1 when all regions, otherwise SEV2.
- Is there a workaround? If yes, SEV3.
- Otherwise SEV4.

## Appendix H: glossary
- **IC**: incident commander, the single decision maker during an incident.
- **Mitigate**: stop customer impact, even if the root cause is not fixed.
- **Postmortem**: written review of an incident; blameless.
- **Handoff**: the Tuesday transfer of primary on-call duty.
- **Secondary**: backup responder paged after the primary times out.
