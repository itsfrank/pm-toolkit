## Fjord vs Collect
Fjord promises EU residency for Warehouse, but Collect receives all SDK traffic in
us-east-1 ([Fjord](../docs/confluence/project-fjord-design.md),
[Collect limits](../docs/confluence/collect-sdk-limits.md)). Owners: Mei Arakawa, Priya
Natarajan. Not raised yet.

## Lookout recordings vs Fjord
Lookout session recordings stay in us-east-1 for 30 days
([guide part 2](../docs/pdf/lookout-admin-guide/part-02.md)), and Fjord puts support tooling
out of scope. Nobody owns the EU answer.
