# Collect EU endpoint prototype (Hack Week 2025)

Hack Week project, 2025-06-16 to 2025-06-20. Team: two Ingestion engineers.

We stood up a single edge receiver in eu-central-1 in the **staging** account and sent test
traffic from the web SDK. It worked for one tenant, with no spool and no router.

**Status: abandoned.** Torn down on 2025-07-15. Not productionised; no customer traffic
ever used it. See the Ingestion plans folder for current work.
