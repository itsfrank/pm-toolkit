# Event inspector: regional sample storage

Status: **Approved** by Hanna Duvall on 2026-09-30. In build. Target: **2027-01-31**.

## Today
When an agent opens the event inspector, up to 5,000 recent raw events are copied to the
shared debug bucket in us-east-1 and kept 30 days. This happens for every tenant, wherever
its Warehouse lives.

## Change
- From 2027-01-31, the inspector writes samples for tenants homed outside us-east-1 to a
  bucket in the tenant's home region (for Frankfurt tenants: eu-central-1). Same 30-day
  lifecycle.
- The shared debug bucket keeps only samples of us-east-1 tenants.
- Agents still work from the US and Lisbon offices; reading samples from another region
  is logged.

## Until then
Samples of Frankfurt-homed tenants keep going to us-east-1.
