# Warehouse retention and storage FAQ

**How long do we keep raw events?** By plan: Free 90 days, Team 13 months, Enterprise
25 months (configurable down to 6 months).

**Where is data stored?** In the tenant's home region. US tenants: us-east-1. Beta
tenants of the Frankfurt region: eu-central-1.

**What about backups?** Snapshots and where their copies go are covered by decisions in the
Infrastructure decision log. Ask Infrastructure before quoting anything to a customer.

**When a workspace is deleted?** Warehouse data is deleted within 30 days.
