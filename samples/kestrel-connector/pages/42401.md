# LP-2026-14: Position on EU residency claims

Status: **In effect** from 2026-09-22. Owner: Ines Moreau (Legal & Privacy).

## Position
1. Kestrel may not say externally that customer data "stays in the EU", or offer "EU data
   residency", until ingestion, support tooling copies and snapshot copies of EU-homed
   tenants all stay in the EU.
2. Until then, external wording is limited to: "EU-homed Warehouse storage, in beta".
3. Any PRD, launch plan or customer answer about residency needs a Legal review by Ines
   Moreau before anything is shared outside Kestrel.
4. The DPA annex listing processing locations must be updated before any residency
   offering reaches GA.

## Why
Customers' auditors check every copy of their data, including transient buffers, support
samples and copies kept for restore.
