# D-2026-17: Cold storage tier for events older than 13 months

Status: Accepted (Tomas Lindqvist, 2026-07-29)

## Context
Raw events older than 13 months are queried less than once a month per tenant on average,
but sit on the same storage class as hot data.

## Decision
Move event partitions older than 13 months to an infrequent-access storage class in the
same region as the tenant's Warehouse. Queries on cold partitions may take up to 3x longer.

## Consequences
- Estimated saving: $180k/year.
- No change to retention periods or to the region data is stored in.
