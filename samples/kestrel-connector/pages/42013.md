# D-2026-18: Query engine autoscaling floor

Status: Accepted (Tomas Lindqvist, 2026-08-21)

## Decision
Keep a floor of 6 query workers per region at all times, including the new Frankfurt
region during beta, so that the first dashboard load of the day stays under 3 seconds.

## Consequences
Adds about $40k/year per region.
