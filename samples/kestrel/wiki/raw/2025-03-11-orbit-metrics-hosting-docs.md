## Hosting regions
Orbit Metrics Cloud is available in two hosting regions: United States and European Union
(Frankfurt). Customers choose a region when the workspace is created.

## What stays in region
"Event data and dashboards for EU workspaces are stored and processed in the EU region."

## Notes
- Region selection is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.
- Support staff may access workspace data with customer permission.
