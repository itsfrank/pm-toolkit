# DRAFT: Enterprise plan repackaging proposal (FY27)

Author: Marco Bellini, Pricing & Packaging. Date: 2026-09-29.
Status: DRAFT for discussion. NOT APPROVED. Do not share outside Pricing & Packaging and
Product leadership.

## Proposal summary
1. Split Enterprise into Enterprise and Enterprise Plus.
2. Move audit log retention beyond 90 days to Enterprise Plus.
3. **EU data residency as a paid add-on** to Enterprise and Enterprise Plus, list price
   proposed at 18% of platform fee, minimum $24k/year.
4. Regional access policies (Govern) bundled in Enterprise Plus.

## Rationale
Second region carries a fixed run-rate cost (about $520k/year per Infrastructure estimate).
Add-on pricing recovers it at about 25 tenants.
