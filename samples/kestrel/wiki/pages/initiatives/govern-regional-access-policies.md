# Regional access policies (RAP)

Govern team initiative: restrict who can access a tenant's data by viewer location, and
restrict Kestrel employee (support, success) access to approved locations. [S-0008]

## Key facts
- Status: in build. Private beta 2026-11 (target); GA 2027 H1 (target, not committed). [S-0008]
- Audit log entry for every policy decision. [S-0008]
- Depends on the tenant home region from Project Fjord metadata. [S-0008]
- Lookout (support console) integration not yet scoped with Support Engineering. [S-0008]
- Packaging undecided. [S-0008]
