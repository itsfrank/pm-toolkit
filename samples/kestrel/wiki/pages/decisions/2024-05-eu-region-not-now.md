# Decision D-2024-07: EU hosting region, not now (2024-05-21)

Product, Engineering and Finance decided not to build an EU hosting region in FY25 because
cost was not justified by four Enterprise requests. [S-0003]

## Key facts
- Options were a full EU region ($1.4M/year plus 3 engineers for 3 quarters), EU storage
  only with US ingestion (Legal objected: "not a residency story customers will accept"),
  or do nothing now. [S-0003]
- Revisit triggers: 10+ qualified Enterprise requests in two quarters; a regulatory driver
  blocking renewals; second-region run-rate below $600k/year. [S-0003]
- Sales may not promise EU hosting. [S-0003]

## People and ownership
- Decided by VP Product (then Raj Mehta), VP Engineering, CFO. [S-0003]
