# Approved answer: Where is customer data hosted?

Approved for external use on 2026-03-10 by Ines Moreau (Legal & Privacy) and Sam Whitlock
(Platform PM). Used in RFPs and security questionnaires.

## Answer text
"Kestrel Analytics hosts all customer data in the United States, in AWS us-east-1. EU
hosting is not currently offered."

## Key facts
- Production runs in AWS us-east-1; no customer-selectable region. [S-0001]
- Sales may not promise EU hosting. [S-0003]
