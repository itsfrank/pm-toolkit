## Summary
2024 decision not to build an EU hosting region in FY25, with cost estimates and explicit
revisit triggers.

## Key facts
- Options: full EU region ($1.4M/year + 3 engineers x 3 quarters), EU storage only (Legal
  objected), do nothing now. Chose do nothing now.
- Reason: cost not justified by four requests.
- Revisit triggers: 10+ qualified Enterprise requests in two quarters; a regulatory driver
  blocking renewals; second-region run-rate below $600k/year.
- Sales may not promise EU hosting.

## Caveats
Two years old. Decision makers have partly changed.
