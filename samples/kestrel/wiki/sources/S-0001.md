## Summary
Internal overview of the five products, owning teams, plans and commonly asked facts.
Basis for most product pages.

## Key facts
- Five products: Collect (ingestion SDKs and endpoints), Warehouse "KW" (storage and query),
  Insights (dashboards), Govern (access control, compliance; beta), Connect (integrations,
  exports).
- Production runs in AWS us-east-1; no customer-selectable region (as of 2025-11).
- Plans: Starter, Growth, Enterprise. Enterprise: SSO, audit log, 13-month retention, 99.9% SLA.
- About 140 Enterprise tenants of about 1,900 paying tenants (as of 2025-10).

## Caveats
Predates Project Fjord and Insights v2 GA. Region statement is stale for EU beta tenants.
