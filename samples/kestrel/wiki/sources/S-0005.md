## Summary
Design for the three-stage ingestion pipeline (edge receivers, spool, router) that rolled
out to all tenants in September 2026.

## Key facts
- Pipeline: edge receivers, durable spool, router that forwards to Warehouse writers.
- Target 400k events/s per cluster, p99 accept latency < 120 ms.
- Rollout 100% on 2026-09-20, no incidents.
