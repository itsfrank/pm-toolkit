## Summary
Owner-written design for an EU home region (AWS eu-central-1) for Kestrel Warehouse.
Region is in beta.

## Key facts
- Scope: Warehouse storage, query engine, tenant metadata for EU-homed tenants.
- Out of scope: Collect, Insights, Connect, support tooling.
- Beta since 2026-09-01, 3 design-partner tenants, new tenants only.
- Target GA 2027-02-15: engineering target, not approved for external communication.
- Run-rate estimate $520k/year.
- Existing-tenant migration not designed.

## Caveats
Says nothing about backup and DR location ("Not covered in this document").
