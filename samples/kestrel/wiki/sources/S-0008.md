## Summary
Govern initiative to restrict data access by viewer location and restrict Kestrel employee
access by location.

## Key facts
- Policies by viewer country; restrictions on employee (support, success) access by location.
- Private beta 2026-11 (target); GA 2027 H1 (target, not committed).
- Depends on Fjord tenant home region; Lookout integration not scoped.
- Packaging undecided.

## Caveats
Targets only.
